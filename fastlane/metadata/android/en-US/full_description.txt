DoubleEntry is personal bookkeeping done the way accountants do it: every transaction is a balanced entry between accounts, so your balance sheet, income statement and net worth always add up.

Your ledger stays on your phone. There is no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

<b>Accounts</b>
• Net worth at a glance, with accounts grouped by type
• A monthly ledger per account with a running balance
• Reconcile an account against a bank statement; later changes to a reconciled period are flagged
• A ready-made chart of accounts to start from

<b>Bank statement import</b>
• Import your bank's CSV/TSV export into an account
• Delimiter, encoding, columns, dates and number formats are detected for you
• Rows already in the ledger are skipped, and new rows are booked like your earlier transactions with the same text

<b>Transactions</b>
• Balanced multi-line entries with auto-balancing
• Suggestions from earlier transactions as you type a title
• Edit, duplicate, and delete with undo
• Search across all months

<b>Recurring transactions</b>
Rent, salary and subscriptions post themselves weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly.

<b>Reports</b>
• Balance sheet, income statement (EBITDA, EBT, spendable net income) and cash flow
• Cost trends chart by group or account
• Month, year-to-date or full year, compared with the previous period or the same period last year

<b>Assets and invoices</b>
• Automatic monthly depreciation of assets
• Invoices with due dates, payments in full or in instalments, and an optional daily reminder

<b>Your data, your way</b>
• Full JSON backup and restore, and CSV export of transactions and balances
• Optional app lock with screen lock or biometrics
• Light and dark theme, optional Material You colors
• A net-worth home-screen widget and launcher shortcuts
