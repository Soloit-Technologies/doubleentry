# The name and icon

DoubleEntry is open source, and you're welcome to fork it, change it and publish your own version.
We'd love to see what you build.

The one thing we ask: if you publish it, give it your own name and icon, and a different
application id, so nobody mistakes it for the official app or installs it over ours. Saying it's
"based on DoubleEntry" is great.

Not sure if something's OK? Just ask at hello@soloit.se.
